# Image Sourcing Policy

Visit Cambodia uses real photographic imagery only.

## Approved sources

- Pexels — free for personal and commercial use under the Pexels license.
- Unsplash — free to use under the Unsplash License, subject to its restrictions.
- Pixabay — free use under the Pixabay Content License, subject to its restrictions.
- Wikimedia Commons — acceptable when the specific file license permits the intended use; retain the source and license information.
- Adobe Stock — allowed only when the project has a valid paid license for the specific asset. Adobe Stock is not treated as a free stock source.

## Rules

1. Do not use AI-generated images, synthetic placeholders, custom SVG illustrations, or unrelated photos as destination photography.
2. Never use a photo of one place while labeling it as a different place.
3. When an image cannot be verified as a real, appropriately licensed photograph, leave the image empty rather than inventing a replacement.
4. Keep source/credit/license information in the content data when attribution or editorial credit is appropriate or required.
5. Prefer original, place-specific photography for destinations, provinces, food, hotels, attractions, and events.
6. Check recognizable people, logos, trademarks, artworks, and other third-party rights before commercial use.

This policy is part of the production content standard for the site.
