# Visit Cambodia roadmap

Updated 5 October 2026

## Phase 1 — Make the site easy to orient in

In progress.

- Give `/` a distinct home page with a clear overview and curated destinations.
- Move destination search and filters to `/explore`.
- Keep the main navigation short. Put destination types, food, travel guides and province links in the Explore dropdown.
- Make page location clear with active navigation and breadcrumbs.

## Phase 2 — Build a reliable destination directory

Next.

- Complete and verify destination records for all provinces and municipality.
- Prioritize official Ministry of Tourism destination information and its current tourism guidebook; retain a source URL and last-checked date for every time-sensitive fact.
- Separate stable editorial descriptions from changing details such as opening hours, prices, access and transport.
- Show a clear “last checked” date and a way to report incorrect information.

## Phase 3 — Add trip-planning information

- Organize practical guidance around trip preparation, regional travel, cultural etiquette, accessibility and visitor services.
- Link to official information for rules and services that can change; avoid presenting old travel details as current.
- Keep the Khmer and English versions aligned and label their update dates.

## Phase 4 — Add timely tourism updates

- Curate official events, advisories and tourism news with links to the original Ministry source.
- Store publication date, source and expiry/review date so outdated notices can be removed.
- Keep time-sensitive announcements separate from evergreen destination guides.

## Phase 5 — Improve discovery using visitor feedback

- Review search terms, empty results and popular destinations to find gaps in coverage.
- Add focused filters only where they help visitors choose, such as province, activity, accessibility or trip needs.
- Check desktop, mobile, keyboard navigation, language switching and search/filter flows before release.

## Official reference sources

- Ministry of Tourism homepage: <https://www.tourism.gov.kh/> — destination discovery, provincial links and official visitor resources.
- Ministry of Tourism guidebook and legal/resources page: <https://tourism.gov.kh/policy-laws> — currently lists the official Kingdom of Cambodia tourism guidebook.
- Ministry of Tourism news and events: <https://tourism.gov.kh/news/app-news-and-media>.

Use these sources for verification and attribution. Do not copy Ministry text or imagery into this site without checking reuse rights.
