# Province research and source policy

Visit Cambodia is intended for real trip planning, so province guidance must be based on sources that can be checked and should avoid invented operational details.

## Source hierarchy

1. Ministry of Tourism of Cambodia and other Cambodian government sources for official destination information.
2. UNESCO World Heritage Centre for heritage designation, historical significance and site-management facts.
3. Conservation organizations such as WWF Cambodia for wildlife and protected-area information.
4. Established Cambodia travel references for practical destination orientation.
5. Wikipedia for cross-checking names, geography and historical context, never as the sole authority for time-sensitive claims.

## Content rules

- Each province should answer: what makes this place different, why a traveler might go, what history or culture matters, and how to plan the visit.
- Do not publish unverified prices, opening hours, transport schedules, hotel availability or business claims as static facts.
- Use the page disclaimer to tell travelers to confirm changing details locally before departure.
- Avoid outdated tourism statistics unless the publication date and source are clear.
- Do not use old demographic percentages or historical claims just because they appear on a legacy tourism page.
- Wildlife content should prioritize current conservation guidance and responsible viewing.
- Remote destinations should state when extra planning, transport time or local guidance is sensible.
- Province source URLs should remain stored with the guide so editorial claims can be audited later.
